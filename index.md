# Fintra Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_ · _Contact: **fintra.studio@gmail.com**_

Fintra ("the app") is a personal budget and expense tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## Summary
- Your budget data stays **on your device**, encrypted.
- We run **no server**, have **no accounts**, show **no ads** and use **no analytics or tracking**.
- Data leaves your device only if **you** turn on Google Drive backup, or export a backup file yourself.

## Data the app handles
**Financial data you enter:** income, expenses, categories, budgets, goals, notes and dates, plus the profile answers from onboarding (such as monthly income and savings percentage). It is stored locally in an encrypted database; the encryption key is held in the Android Keystore.

**Preferences:** language, currency, budget period day, hidden-amounts mode, reminder settings. Stored locally.

**Camera / receipt photos:** if you scan a receipt, the photo is processed **on your device** by Google ML Kit text and barcode recognition to pre-fill the amount, date and type. The photo is **not saved by Fintra and not sent anywhere** by Fintra.

**Notifications:** if you enable payment reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Google Drive backup (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google, Fintra stores a copy of your data in your own Google Drive, in the hidden **application data folder** that only Fintra can access. This lets you keep several of your devices in sync and restore your data on a new device. We (the developer) cannot see, access or download this data. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Fintra requests only the Drive app-data permission and your sign-in identity needed to connect; it does not read your other Drive files.

You can stop syncing by signing out in the app, and delete the backup by removing Fintra's app data in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps with account access).

## Backup files you export
The JSON backup you export is a plain, readable file saved where you choose or shared through an app you choose. Keep it somewhere safe; it is not encrypted.

## Sharing
We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. The only third parties involved are Google (Drive, only if you enable it; ML Kit, which runs on-device) and Google Play (installing the app, and purchases if a paid plan is offered in the future).

## Retention and deletion
Data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes local data. In the app you can delete individual records or restart onboarding to erase everything locally. Drive backup data is removed as described above.

## Children
Fintra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Security
Local data is encrypted at rest; Android backup of app data is disabled so your financial data is not copied to cloud backups automatically. No method is perfectly secure, and if you lose the device key (for example by a factory reset without a Drive or file backup) data cannot be recovered.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above is updated and, for material changes, the app will tell you.
